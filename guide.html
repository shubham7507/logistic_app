Before you start

This is a working prototype that runs entirely in your browser.

Your own private demo

Everything you do is saved only in this browser. Nobody else sees it, and another device or incognito window starts fresh.

Checks are simulated

GST, PAN, bank, DigiLocker, payments and SMS behave like the real services but nothing is sent anywhere. Test values are shown on each form and in Try it yourself.

One person plays every role

In real life the customer, shop owner, worker and MoveAI reviewer are different people on different phones. In the demo you switch between them from the workspace menu or the ▶ Tours & testing button.

Who uses MoveAI

Four apps share one account: MoveAI (customers), MoveAI Partner (drivers, helpers, delivery partners), MoveAI Business (shops, logistics businesses and their staff) and MoveAI Admin (MoveAI's own team). A person can hold several roles and switch between them.

Journeys, start to finish

Each video was recorded automatically from the live demo. Show me in the app plays the same journey inside the real app with captions; Step by step lets you go at your own pace. Starting a journey resets the demo in your browser.

How the rules work

The checks that protect customers and honest sellers, in plain words.

Seller review by risk

  • Low risk (household or fashion, every check passes): approved automatically and goes live on probation.
  • Food or electricals: a MoveAI reviewer checks each section.
  • Anything flagged (bank name mismatch, no GST, the same PAN used by someone else): reviewed, plus a video call.
  • The pincode must be in the same state as the GSTIN.

Strict states (Bihar now)

  • Aadhaar through DigiLocker (only a masked number is kept), a live selfie matched to it, and geo-tagged shop photos.
  • Food and electricals also need distributor invoices and a field visit; flagged sellers a police certificate.
  • MoveAI can switch any state to strict, with a written reason.

Probation for new shops

  • No cash on delivery, a cap on order value (₹3,000; ₹2,000 in Bihar) and a product limit (50; 30 in Bihar).
  • Payouts held 7 extra days (14 in Bihar).
  • Lifts after 30 delivered orders and 30 days with no strikes (50 orders in Bihar).

Payments

  • Customers pay MoveAI, never the shop directly; the money is held until delivery.
  • Only the bank can confirm a payment. Unpaid orders cancel after 15 minutes.
  • The shop sees "waiting for payment — don't pack yet" until then.

Expiry and strikes

  • Packaged food carries batch, manufacture and expiry dates.
  • Food must reach customers with at least 45 days or 30% of its shelf life left; older stock is hidden automatically.
  • A customer can report an expired item with a photo: instant refund, product paused, and a strike for the shop. Three strikes suspend the shop.

Delivery choices

  • MoveAI couriers, the shop's own staff, or customer pickup — and combinations (own staff nearby, couriers further away).
  • Coverage is checked by pincode; each option shows the cost and how COD works.
  • Changes after going live are reviewed by MoveAI.

Staff joining

  • The owner invites by mobile; the worker gets an SMS/WhatsApp link.
  • They confirm with an OTP, verify Aadhaar, take a selfie and add a bank account; the owner approves.
  • Workers check in each day and receive orders automatically.

Questions

Is this the real app?

It is a clickable prototype of the real flows. It has no server: real launches need the backend, real KYC and payment providers, and SMS/WhatsApp.

Why did my data disappear?

Starting a journey or "Restore full demo" resets the demo in your browser. Data is also separate per browser and device.

How do I approve my own seller application?

Press ▶ Tours & testing → Open admin console (testing). In real life a MoveAI reviewer does this on their own login, and nobody can review their own application.

Can I show it without the testing buttons?

Yes: add ?demo=off to the link (for example index.html?demo=off).
